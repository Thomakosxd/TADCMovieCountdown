Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about The Last Act

The Last Act is the series finale of The Amazing Digital Circus, a hit indie animated web series created by Gooseworx and produced by Glitch Productions. It combines Episode 8 with the all-new, hour-long Episode 9 — the grand conclusion to the entire story — presented as a feature-length theatrical event before its YouTube and Netflix debut.

The theatrical event runs from June 4–18, 2026 across select countries worldwide. The US run is June 4–7 (with some markets extending to June 18). The standalone episode releases on YouTube and Netflix on June 19, 2026.

US tickets are sold through Fathom Entertainment at fathomentertainment.com. The event is showing at 2,000+ theaters nationwide. Tickets also available on Fandango.

Over 40 countries are confirmed including the United States, Canada, Mexico, Brazil, Argentina, Chile, Colombia, Peru, the UK, Australia, New Zealand, South Africa, and many more across Central America, the Caribbean, Europe, and Asia. Check the release tracker above or visit digitalcircus.movie for a full country list.

The Last Act includes Episode 8 as a recap lead-in, so some newcomers may be able to follow along. However, for the full emotional payoff of the finale, watching all eight previous episodes (available free on YouTube) is strongly recommended.
